# Infrastructure

Rules for the adapters, kept out of the root `CLAUDE.md` because they only matter here.

- **Diagnostic journal** (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-journal-diagnostic-design.md`): kept apart from learning data, in its own IndexedDB (`neckspert-play-diagnostics`), never in the backup. Only adapters write to it (`DiagnosticSink`: `WebAudioInput`, `DiagnosticAudioRecorder` at 16 kHz, `NnlsChordVerifier` with expectations, full reports and spectra); the core and the mechanics never see it. A take runs from the input's opening to its closing; joins with attempts are made by time at analysis. `Settings.diagnostics` (`enabled: null` = on in dev builds, off in published ones; budget in MB, oldest closed takes pruned). Export: a STORE zip built take by take (`exportArchive.ts`). `ChordReport` carries no spectrum: attempts stay small.
- **Audio input** is opened raw (no echo cancellation / noise suppression / AGC), except the Firefox-on-macOS built-in mic workaround in `WebAudioNoteDetector`. The click and every scheduled sound are pitchless filtered noise so the note detector reads no pitch in them alone; over a note that rings, the click heard on the playing input reads as that note again, which is why the start screen asks for headphones (`asksForHeadphones`); there is no verdict sound (removed with `SoundCuePort` in H5).
- **Played notes** are two independent pieces combined: the attacks of the attack sensor (`WebAudioAttackDetector`, which knows nothing of pitch) and the pitch read in the two windows after each one (`NoteTracker.attack`, `readPitch`, `confirmedPitch`, which know nothing of attacks). A note is told some 150 ms after its attack at most, within `LIVE_READING_MARGIN_S`. The RMS rule of `OnsetDetector` (1.8 times a slow envelope) lost a note in two when the previous one still sounded (exports of a piece, 2026-10-31: 68/125 at ♩ = 70, 52/125 at ♩ = 93; now 125/125); only the chord verifier still uses it. The tracker reports what sounds and forgives nothing: a string left ringing by a lifted finger or touched by accident is a note, and an extra note fails a round, because cleanliness is what the player works on. What an exercise tolerates belongs to its judge or its data, not to the sensor. Hearing a note without an attack (slide, bend, legato) would take a continuous pitch sensor, which does not exist.
