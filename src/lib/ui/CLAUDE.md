# UI

## Visual system

`DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` describe the new system (« Près du silence »), built from the code. Its tokens are the `:root` block of `src/lib/ui/tokens.css` after the Game Boy one (`--np-ground`, `--np-text`, `--np-earth`/`--np-muted` for rules and large text, `--np-earth-text`/`--np-muted-text` for text at body and meta sizes, `--np-now` amber only for « now »: the now line, the active rail destination, the current block and the one main action of a screen, `--np-field`, `--np-on-field`, `--np-size-*`, `--np-step-*`, `--np-cell*`, `--np-motion-*`), and `html, body` are painted in it on every page; never use raw values. Shared primitives live in `src/lib/ui/primitives/` (Button with `primary|quiet|choice`, InfoLine, VerdictMark + `verdictTone`, Lane/LaneCell, Prompt, NavRail, Page, BlockList) plus the mode-2 `AnswerKeyboard`. The shell (`src/routes/+layout.svelte`) shows the rail and the light world only off play (`src/lib/ui/shell/navigation.ts`); screens off play sit in `Page`. Migrated: the play screen of notes and triads, the shell, home, summary, settings, calibration, neck map. `PlayFrame` takes the world of its view (`VIEW_WORLDS`, `ui/play/views/index.ts`): the notes' and the triads' views (`lane`, `answer-keys`) and the figure view (`figure`: the score in mist on the field, the cursor amber, the counted bar in the prompt's row, the figure to play kept in its place at the breath with the run played under it, `PlayedCycle`) and their frame are quiet, their chrome `QuietField`, `QuietTop`, `QuietOverlay`; a quiet view adds its parts to the frame's info line through `useInfoLine()` (`ui/play/infoLine.svelte.ts`: the figure view gives its exercise, its level, its bar and « silence » in a muted bar) and shows them itself without a frame; the frame lays a level reached over the top of the field, so a view whose content starts at the top keeps that band free (`FigureView`'s top padding). The gesture views (`grid-scroll`, `link-lane`) and their frame still use the Game Boy tokens (VT323 too), declared only under `:root:has([data-world='pixel'])` so they exist only while a pixel frame is on screen, until plan 3 (`tokens.spec.ts` lists the files allowed to read them); `ChordShape`, `Term` and `NeckDiagram` are themed in migrated screens through `--shape-*`, `--term-*` and `--neck-*`, the Game Boy tokens their fallbacks. One screen never mixes the two worlds; new screens use the new tokens only. Play-screen rules: no explanatory words in play, the verdict shown on the lane by shape, text at least 16px; the direction contract is `.impeccable/surfaces/src-routes-session-page-svelte.md`. `prefers-reduced-motion` drops the imprint and the scene rise.
