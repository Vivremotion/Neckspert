# UI

## Visual system

`DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` describe the visual system (« Près du silence »), the only one since plan 3, built from the code. Its tokens are the `:root` block of `src/lib/ui/tokens.css` (`--np-ground`, `--np-text`, `--np-earth`/`--np-muted` for rules and large text, `--np-earth-text`/`--np-muted-text` for text at body and meta sizes, `--np-now` amber only for « now »: the now line, the active rail destination, the current block and the one main action of a screen, `--np-field`, `--np-on-field`, `--np-size-*`, `--np-step-*`, `--np-cell*`, `--np-motion-*`), and `html, body` are painted in it on every page; never use raw values. Shared primitives live in `src/lib/ui/primitives/` (Button with `primary|quiet|choice`, InfoLine, VerdictMark + `verdictTone`, Lane/LaneCell, Prompt, NavRail, Page, BlockList) plus the mode-2 `AnswerKeyboard`. The shell (`src/routes/+layout.svelte`) shows the rail and the light world only off play (`src/lib/ui/shell/navigation.ts`); screens off play sit in `Page`. Every screen is in it. `PlayFrame` is one frame for every play view (`PLAY_VIEWS`, `ui/play/views/index.ts`): the field (`QuietField`), the info line (`QuietTop`), the light pages of start, pause and end (`QuietOverlay`, with « Réécouter » on the pause for a session that replays), the level told in a band over the top of the field (`--play-told-band`, declared on the stage: a view whose content starts at the top keeps it free, as `FigureView` does). A view adds its parts and its tempo to the info line through `useInfoLine()` (`ui/play/infoLine.svelte.ts`: the figure view gives its exercise, its level, its bar and « silence » in a muted bar, and its tempo with its beat's unit; the chart gives its title) and shows them itself without a frame. The views: `lane` and `answer-keys` (the notes, the triads), `figure` (the score in mist on the field, the cursor amber, the counted bar in the prompt's row, the figure to play kept in its place at the breath with the run played under it, `PlayedCycle`), `link-lane` (the link on the notes' `Lane`: a tile per beat, the way on the first beat of a bar as the tile's own `label`, the chord as the hero prompt, the counted bar above it), `grid-scroll` (the chart as rows of bars, the chord on show as the hero, the next with an arrow, the cells in the lane's tones with `VerdictMark`). `ChordShape` and `Term` are themed by their screen through `--shape-*` and `--term-*`; `NeckDiagram` and `DrawnMiss` read the field's tokens. The Game Boy tokens, VT323, Oxanium, the plates and the pixel primitives are gone (`tokens.spec.ts` refuses their return). Play-screen rules: no explanatory words in play, the verdict shown on the lane by shape, text at least 16px; the direction contract is `.impeccable/surfaces/src-routes-session-page-svelte.md`. `prefers-reduced-motion` drops the imprint and the scene rise.
