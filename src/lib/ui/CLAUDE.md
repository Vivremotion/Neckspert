# UI

## Visual system

`DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` describe the new system (« Près du silence »), built from the code. Its tokens are the `:root` block of `src/lib/ui/tokens.css` after the Game Boy one (`--np-ground`, `--np-text`, `--np-earth`/`--np-muted` for rules and large text, `--np-earth-text`/`--np-muted-text` for text at body and meta sizes, `--np-now` amber only for « now »: the now line, the active rail destination, the current block and the one main action of a screen, `--np-field`, `--np-on-field`, `--np-size-*`, `--np-step-*`, `--np-cell*`, `--np-motion-*`), and `html, body` are painted in it on every page; never use raw values. Shared primitives live in `src/lib/ui/primitives/` (Button with `primary|quiet|choice`, InfoLine, VerdictMark + `verdictTone`, Lane/LaneCell, Prompt, NavRail, Page, BlockList) plus the mode-2 `AnswerKeyboard`. The shell (`src/routes/+layout.svelte`) shows the rail and the light world only off play (`src/lib/ui/shell/navigation.ts`); screens off play sit in `Page`. Migrated: the play screen of notes and triads, the shell, home, summary, settings, calibration, neck map. `PlayFrame` takes the world of its view (`VIEW_WORLDS`, `ui/play/views/index.ts`): the notes' and the triads' views (`lane`, `answer-keys`) and their frame are quiet, their chrome `QuietField`, `QuietTop`, `QuietOverlay`; the gesture and rhythm views and their frame still use the Game Boy tokens (VT323 too), declared only under `:root:has([data-world='pixel'])` so they exist only while a pixel frame is on screen, until plan 3 (`tokens.spec.ts` lists the files allowed to read them); `ChordShape`, `Term` and `PlayedCycle` are themed in migrated screens through `--shape-*`, `--term-*` and a commented local bridge. One screen never mixes the two worlds; new screens use the new tokens only. Play-screen rules: no explanatory words in play, the verdict shown on the lane by shape, text at least 16px; the direction contract is `.impeccable/surfaces/src-routes-session-page-svelte.md`. `prefers-reduced-motion` drops the imprint and the scene rise.
