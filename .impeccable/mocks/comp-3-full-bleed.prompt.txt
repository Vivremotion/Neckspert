Flat front-on UI design comp of a landscape 16:9 desktop web app screen: the PLAY SCREEN of "Neckspert Play" during a series, just after a note was played, read from 1 metre by a guitarist whose hands are on the guitar. Keep the pixel-art system grammar of the reference image exactly (8x8 tile grid, 8x8 pixel cap font, crisp square pixels, hierarchy by border boxes and inversion, checkerboard dither) and its four-tone palette; change the COMPOSITION: FULL BLEED, no letterbox, the tile grid covers the entire 16:9 viewport (a wide 240x135 logical-pixel field integer-scaled).

Structure, in order:
1. Top strip, full width, a double-line pixel border box: left "MESURE 2/2", right "TEMPS 3/4" followed by exactly four square beat pips (three filled, one hollow). Small.
2. Left 60% of the field, dominant: the consigne. A giant blocky pixel letter "F" built from whole tiles, about two thirds of the viewport height, with a one-tile shade drop shadow; beneath it "corde de B" in the pixel font at triple scale.
3. Right 40%, a tall bordered box: the VERDICT, the focal secondary. At top a large pixel sprite of a right-pointing arrow (the En retard shape), below it "EN RETARD" large in pixel font inside an inverted band, then "+95 ms" and "1,21 s" in pixel digits.
4. Bottom strip, full width, thin: a row of eight small 8x8 verdict sprites for the last attempts of the series, left to right, each a distinct shape readable without colour: filled circle (Parfait), left arrow (En avance), right arrow (En retard), cross X (Mauvaise note); sequence: circle, circle, left arrow, circle, cross, circle, circle, right arrow, the last one inverted as current.

Palette strictly four flat tones: ground #E99568 peach-amber, midtone #BE6754, shade #813B2A, ink #451218 deep oxblood; any fifth tone only as a checkerboard dither of two of these. Text exactly and only: "MESURE 2/2", "TEMPS 3/4", "F", "corde de B", "EN RETARD", "+95 ms", "1,21 s". French interface. No navigation, no logo, no score, no XP, no streaks, no guitar imagery, no wood, no strings, no drawn fretboard, no neon glow, no CRT scanlines, no gradients.
Reference image: .impeccable/mocks/decision/challenger-gameboy-bonfire.png. Post-process: colours snapped to the measured Bonfire palette with .impeccable/tools/recolor.py.
